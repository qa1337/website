Device fragmentation

Fragmentation is the main risk on Android. Plan around what varies.

What varies

Dimension What to vary
Screen Small phone, large phone, tablet, foldable
OS version Latest, and the oldest you support
Manufacturer skin Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi
Memory Low-RAM devices

Device checklist

  • Cover at least one Samsung and one Pixel device.
  • Test with battery saver on and off.
  • Verify permissions on first launch and after revoke.
