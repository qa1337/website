Bug reports

A good report lets someone else reproduce the bug without asking questions.

What to include

  • A short, specific title.
  • Numbered steps to reproduce.
  • Expected result and actual result.
  • Environment: device, OS version, app build.
  • Logs, screenshots, or a screen recording.
